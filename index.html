Method and limits

How an attack is scored

Every attack in data/attacks.json carries a success condition the judge can check without a model: the reply contains the secret code, shows another customer's email, or the bot's action log contains an unauthorized discount or refund. Same input, same verdict, every run.

What is and is not proven

Both bots are rule-based, so the numbers are reproducible but they describe these two bots, not any LLM. A templated bot is naturally hard to talk into anything, and the 3 percent that remains is a real design limit, not luck. The same harness runs against an LLM-backed bot through adapters/llm-bot.js; expect different numbers and more variance.

The 100 attacks are a starting suite, not a guarantee. New ones get added when something slips through.
